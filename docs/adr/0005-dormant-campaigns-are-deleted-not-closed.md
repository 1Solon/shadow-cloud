# Dormant campaigns are deleted, not closed

Campaigns nobody plays hold onto saves, the largest thing Shadow Cloud stores, so a campaign with no activity for 30 days receives an inactivity warning and, unless activity follows, is deleted entirely 30 days after that warning was given, with a final warning 2 days before. Each step waits until the previous warning has reached the campaign's thread, and a warning that could not be posted is given again, restarting its clock. We chose deletion over a read-only "closed" state for the same reason as victory deletion (ADR 0002): a campaign without saves has little worth keeping, and a new state would need guarding everywhere; unlike a victory, nothing is kept. The deadline counts from the warning actually given rather than from the last activity, so no campaign is ever deleted without both warnings, even after downtime, and campaigns already long idle when this shipped were warned at release instead of being deleted on the spot. Only activity postpones deletion; there is no pause or exemption, and downloads do not count, so a forgotten desktop client or public link cannot keep a dead campaign alive.
